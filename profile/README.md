## ⚡ What is SettleX?

SettleX is the **clearing layer for the decentralized economy**. We bridge assets across any blockchain securely and efficiently, cutting bridging costs by up to **90%**.

Instead of bridging every transaction individually, SettleX aggregates cross-chain flows and settles only the **net delta**. Designed for Market Makers, Solvers, and Intent Networks, we maximize capital efficiency where it matters most.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 📉 Multilateral Netting
We don't bridge everything. We aggregate flows and settle the difference. This drastically reduces on-chain volume and cuts gas costs by up to 90%.

### 🌐 Chain-Agnostic Compatibility
We support major blockchains out of the box. Our modular framework allows us to deploy to **any new chain in under 24 hours**.

### 🛡️ Compliance-Ready Architecture
Future-proof by design. Our infrastructure is modular, built to seamlessly integrate institutional compliance and regulatory reporting standards as the landscape evolves.

### 🔒 Privacy-Ready Design
Built for institutional discretion. Enable **selective decryption** to protect your trading strategies without compromising auditability.

### 🧩 Plug-and-Play Integration
* **For Devs:** Integrate via our **SDK** or **API** to route settlements automatically.
* **For Traders:** Use our standard UI for manual control.
